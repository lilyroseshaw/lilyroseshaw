California Data Broker Cleanup (DROP)

Cookie Monster can identify companies in your personal digital footprint — the ones you actually gave your information to directly. California's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) can separately submit deletion requests to registered data brokers (companies that collect and sell data about you without a direct relationship).

Cookie Monster does not replace DROP — they cover different, complementary parts of your digital footprint.
